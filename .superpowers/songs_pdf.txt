5) 12 BAR BLUES FORMULA 
 
The 12-bar blues is one of the most popular in pop music. It originates in blues songs but 
countless rock songs also use the formula. 
 
 
This is the pattern using E-A-B, then  1-4-5 
 
     E     E     E    E 
     A     A     E    E 
     B     A     E    E A B 
 
(Play each chord 4 times) 
 
     1     1     1     1 
     4     4     1     1 
     5     4     1     1 
It is played using the 1-4-5 formula in the pattern written out below.  
The combinations of chords are below. In the following list, songs with asterisks (*) need bar chords. 
C              F    G* 
D              G    A 
E              A    B 
F              Bb   C* 
G              C    D 
A              D    E 
B              E    F#* 
1-4-5 All - These songs can be played with Just Open Chords 
C              F    G 
D              G    A 
G              C    D 
A               D    E
