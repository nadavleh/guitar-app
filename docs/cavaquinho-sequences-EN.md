# Chord Sequences for Cavaquinho — Basic & Intermediate

*English translation of **"Sequências de acordes para cavaquinho — nível básico e médio"** by
**Betto Correa** (cavaquinho player, graduate of the ULM — Universidade Livre de Música, São Paulo).*

> **Translator's / app note**
> - This app uses the **traditional samba tuning DGBD** (highest string tuned to **D** instead of E)
>   as the cavaquinho default. All chord shapes in the original booklet assume this tuning.
> - In the original, fret numbers such as *5th, 7th, 9th* refer to the fret on the cavaquinho neck
>   where the **barre (pestana)** or starting note begins.
> - Every sequence below is also playable interactively in **Instrument → Cavaquinho → Progressions**,
>   where you can transpose to any key and scroll voice-led shapes along the neck.

## About this material

This material contains the essential sequences you need to become a practical player. You will learn
the sequences and the **II–V–I** cadences in major and minor. Don't get hung up on the individual
chords — pay attention to the **structure of the harmony**. Once these sequences are memorised, you can
use the companion booklet *ACORDES DISSONANTES* (dissonant chords) to substitute the plain chords with
**dissonant** ones.

Portuguese ↔ English note names: **Dó = C, Ré = D, Mi = E, Fá = F, Sol = G, Lá = A, Si = B.**

## Module 01 — Basic Major Sequences

Every basic major sequence follows the same functional pattern — a **"quadradinho"**:

> **I – VI7 – ii – V7**

| # | Key (PT → EN) | Chords |
|---|---------------|--------|
| 01 | Dó Maior → C major | **C – A7 – Dm – G7** |
| 02 | Ré Maior → D major | **D – B7 – Em – A7** |
| 03 | Mi Maior → E major | **E – C♯7 – F♯m – B7** |
| 04 | Fá Maior → F major | **F – D7 – Gm – C7** |
| 05 | Sol Maior → G major | **G – E7 – Am – D7** |
| 06 | Lá Maior → A major | **A – F♯7 – Bm – E7** |
| 07 | Si Maior → B major | **B – G♯7 – C♯m – F♯7** |

## Module 01 — Basic Minor Sequences

Every basic minor sequence follows the pattern:

> **i – I7 – iv – V7**

| # | Key (PT → EN) | Chords |
|---|---------------|--------|
| 01 | Dó Menor → C minor | **Cm – C7 – Fm – G7** |
| 02 | Ré Menor → D minor | **Dm – D7 – Gm – A7** |
| 03 | Mi Menor → E minor | **Em – E7 – Am – B7** |
| 04 | Fá Menor → F minor | **Fm – F7 – B♭m – C7** |
| 05 | Sol Menor → G minor | **Gm – G7 – Cm – D7** |
| 06 | Lá Menor → A minor | **Am – A7 – Dm – E7** |
| 07 | Si Menor → B minor | **Bm – B7 – Em – F♯7** |

## Module 02 — Intermediate ("Médio") Major Sequences

The intermediate major sequence extends the basic one into a full 13-chord phrase:

> **I – VI7 – ii – V7 – v – I7 – IV – iv – iii – VI7 – ii – V7 – I**

Realised in **C major**:

> **C – A7 – Dm – G7 – Gm – C7 – F – Fm – Em – A7 – Dm – G7 – C**

The same functional line is given in the original for all seven keys (Ré, Mi, Fá, Sol, Lá, Si). Transpose
the pattern, or use the app's **Progressions** screen and change the key.

## Module 02 — Intermediate ("Médio") Minor Sequences

The intermediate minor sequences (Dó, Ré, Mi, Fá, Sol, Lá, Si menor) expand the basic
**i – I7 – iv – V7** minor cadence with passing dominants and the relative major, in the same spirit as
the intermediate major line above.

## II–V–I Cadence Through All Keys (Major)

A continuous drill that chains **ii – V7 – I** cadences and then steps down to the next key
(each resolution's parallel minor becomes the next **ii**):

> **Dm – G7 – C** → **Cm – F7 – B♭** → **B♭m – E♭7 – A♭** → **A♭m – D♭7 – G♭** → **F♯m – B7 – E** → …

Practising this takes the **ii–V–I** motion through every tonal centre.

## II–V–I Cadence Through All Keys (Minor)

The same idea using the **minor** ii–V–i cadence (iiø – V7 – i), chained through all keys.

## Chord Substitutions

In the companion booklet *acordes_cavaquinho* you'll find many dissonant chords for substitutions.
The obvious rule: **any dominant-7th chord (C7, D7, …) may be replaced by a richer dominant** —
**C7/9, C7/9♭, C7/9♯, C7(13)**, etc. — because they are all dominants.
